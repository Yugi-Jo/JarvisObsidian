## Google Maps live busyness — three conditions, not one

Signed in, the busyness data still didn't appear until two more things were right:

1. **The place page, not the search list.** A search URL renders a list; list entries carry
   no busyness. Clicking the list row often only highlights it — click the
   `/maps/place/` anchor.
2. **Scroll the panel.** Maps lazily renders panel sections, so the graph does not exist
   until it scrolls into view. Real wheel events over the panel force it.
3. **Match `LIVE` case-sensitively.** A case-insensitive pattern matches the "livery" in
   **Delivery** and returns junk that reads like an answer.

Live readings that came back for Ikebukuro at ~2:50pm JST: **cafe & bal No.8 co. "not too
busy"**, Doutor "less busy than usual", Excelsior Caffé and Starbucks west exit "a little
busy". Books and Coffee Fukuroshosabo and Cafe de Paris carry **no** Google busyness data at
all — worth saying plainly rather than presenting a guess as a reading.

## Correction: the crossover format needs documented designer homage

Joel caught the flaw in the research's crossover shortlist: "the ideas behind this was that the
card designer was specifically inspired by the other series". He is right, and it is the
load-bearing rule for the format. `Do X Fans Recognize These Yu-Gi-Oh Cards?` only works if
real cards exist whose design references that fandom — otherwise there is nothing for the fans
to recognise and no video.

Verified against the Yu-Gi-Oh wiki and community sources:

- **Masked HERO = Kamen Rider**, with a card-by-card mapping (Anki→Hibiki, Dark Law→Kiva/OOO,
  Goka→Kuuga/Agito/Kabuto, Blast→Rider 1 & 2, Divine Wind→Double). **Inzektor = Kabuto.**
- **Super Quant = Super Sentai / Power Rangers** — wiki-confirmed, Rangers and Zords, with the
  mapping listed (Blue Layer→HurricaneBlue, Green Layer→GingaGreen, Red Layer→GaoRed).
- **Kaiju = kaiju films, not Godzilla only** — Gadarla→Mothra, Gameciel→Gamera, Jizukiru→Legion,
  Radian→Rodan, Dogoran→Godzilla, Mecha Dogoran→Mechagodzilla.
- **Kozmo = Star Wars + Wizard of Oz**, the best-documented of the lot, card by card.
- Ultraman via Neo-Spacian/Neos and Metal Hero via Vision HERO are claimed but not confirmed.

**Dead for this format:** Sonic, Ben 10, Bakugan, Digimon, Pokémon, Toonami — no official homage
exists, only fan-made customs.

The distinction that saves half the ideas: Ben 10 and Sonic still work in the **argument**
format (`BEN 10 Is A Kamen Rider!`, 3,393 views at 31.3%), which needs a defensible comparison
rather than designer intent. The research had blurred the two formats; Joel separated them.

Written into `crossover-landscape.md` (as a correction ahead of the original report),
`pipeline.md` (the crossover slot rewritten, Sonic and Ben 10 removed) and
`references/yugi-jo-config.md` so Nova cannot pitch an invalid pairing again.
